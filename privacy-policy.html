1. Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Delivery address (street, city, state, pincode)
  • Payment method preference (UPI, card, COD — we do NOT store card numbers)
  • Communications you send us via email or WhatsApp

Information Collected Automatically

  • Browser type, device type, operating system
  • IP address and approximate location (city-level)
  • Pages visited, time spent, referring URL
  • Shopping cart contents (stored locally on your device)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfil your orders — including sending order confirmations and delivery updates
  • Contact you about your order via phone or WhatsApp
  • Respond to customer service requests and enquiries
  • Send promotional communications (only with your consent; you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Improve our website and product offerings through usage analytics
  • Comply with legal obligations under applicable Indian law

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only with:

  • Delivery partners — to ship your order (name, address, phone only)
  • Payment gateways — to process payments securely (governed by their own privacy policies)
  • Legal authorities — if required by law, court order, or government regulation

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal, essential cookies to:

  • Remember your shopping cart (stored in browser localStorage)
  • Understand how visitors use our site (anonymous analytics only)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect functionality.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and not share your account details.

We never store card or bank details on our servers. All payment transactions are processed by certified payment gateway partners.

6. Your Rights

Under Indian data protection law (IT Act 2000 and DPDP Act 2023), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time

To exercise these rights, email us at breakfit0@gmail.com

7. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
