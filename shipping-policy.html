3. Delivery Timelines

Order Processing

Orders placed before 2 PM (IST) on business days are processed the same day. Orders after 2 PM or on Sundays/public holidays are processed the next business day.

Mumbai & MMR Region

1–2 business days after dispatch

Metro Cities (Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata)

2–3 business days after dispatch

Tier 2/3 Cities & Remote Areas

3–5 business days after dispatch

Delivery Delays

Timelines may be affected by natural calamities, public holidays, courier strikes, or remote area accessibility. BreakFit is not responsible for delays caused by courier partners, but we will proactively update you.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via WhatsApp/SMS on your registered mobile number. You can track your order on the respective courier partner's website.

5. Packaging

All products are packed in food-grade, vacuum-sealed or zip-lock pouches to preserve freshness during transit. Fragile or premium products (e.g., gift boxes) are bubble-wrapped and placed in rigid outer boxes.

We do not reuse packaging and ensure every order leaves our warehouse in pristine condition.

6. Failed Delivery Attempts

If a delivery attempt fails (e.g., you are unavailable), the courier will make 2 additional attempts. After 3 failed attempts, the package is returned to us. Re-shipping charges of ₹99 will apply for re-delivery of returned orders.
