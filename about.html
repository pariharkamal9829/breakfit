How We Started

A Passion for Pure, Natural Nutrition

In 2021, the founders of R & B Foods noticed something frustrating — the dry fruits sold in most stores were months old, loaded with preservatives, and often mislabelled. They decided to fix that.

"We wanted to create a brand where every package you open feels like it came straight from the farm — because it practically does."

BreakFit started as a small venture sourcing directly from almond orchards in California and cashew farms on the Konkan coast. Word spread quickly. Customers could taste the difference — the freshness, the genuine flavour, the honest labelling.

Today we ship across India, serving families, fitness enthusiasts, and gourmet kitchens that refuse to settle for anything less than the real thing — all from our Mumbai base at Kalbadevi.

Our Core Values

100% Natural

No artificial preservatives, colours, or flavours — ever. What you taste is exactly what nature intended.

We work directly with farmers, cutting out middlemen. Better prices for you, better livelihood for them.

Quality Guaranteed
