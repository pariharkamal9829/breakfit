How We Started

A Passion for Pure, Natural Nutrition

In 2019, our founder Arjun Mehta noticed something frustrating — the dry fruits sold in most stores were months old, loaded with preservatives, and often mislabelled. He decided to fix that.

BreakFit started as a small venture sourcing directly from almond orchards in California and cashew farms in coastal Karnataka. Word spread quickly. Customers could taste the difference — the freshness, the genuine flavour, the honest labelling.

Today we ship across India, serving families, fitness enthusiasts, and gourmet kitchens that refuse to settle for anything less than the real thing.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Everything we do is guided by four principles that we never compromise on.

100% Natural

No artificial preservatives, colours, or flavours — ever. What you taste is exactly what nature intended.

Direct Sourcing

We work directly with farmers, cutting out middlemen. Better prices for you, better livelihood for them.

Quality Guaranteed

Every batch is tested for freshness, moisture content, and purity before it reaches our warehouse.

Sustainable Practices

We partner only with farms that follow responsible agriculture. Good food should be good for the planet too.
