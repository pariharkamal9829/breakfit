Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025

Important Notice for Food Products

In compliance with FSSAI regulations and standard food safety practices, BreakFit does not accept returns or exchanges on any food products once the order is delivered, except in specific circumstances listed below. This policy is necessary to maintain the safety and quality of our products for all customers.

1. No Return / No Exchange Policy

As a food product company regulated under FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India), we strictly do not accept returns or replacements on any dry fruits, nuts, or food items once:

  • The package has been delivered to the customer
  • The seal/packaging has been opened or tampered with
  • More than 48 hours have passed since delivery

This is a food safety requirement and applies to all items in our catalogue — cashews, almonds, walnuts, raisins, dates, and mixed nuts.

Why no returns? Food products, once leaving our controlled environment, can be exposed to contaminants, allergens, or improper storage conditions. Accepting returns would compromise the safety of the product for other customers. This is standard practice for all FSSAI-registered food businesses in India.

2. Exceptions — When We Do Process Refunds

We will process a full refund (no replacement) only in the following strictly verified circumstances:

Wrong Item Delivered

You received a product different from what you ordered (e.g., walnuts instead of almonds).

Damaged / Tampered Package

Package arrived visibly damaged or tampered with at the time of delivery.

Missing Items

Your order was incomplete — some items from your order were not delivered.

Non-Delivery

Your order was marked as delivered but you never received it (after verification).

Change of Mind

You simply changed your mind after receiving the product. Not eligible.

Taste / Preference

You dislike the taste or texture of the product. Not eligible.

3. How to Raise a Refund Claim

If your situation qualifies under Section 2 above, follow these steps within 48 hours of delivery:

  1. Take clear photographs of the received product and packaging.
  2. WhatsApp the photos and your Order ID to +91 9323242591 or email breakfit0@gmail.com.
  3. Our team will review your claim within 24 business hours.
  4. If approved, the refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.
Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days. UPI/bank transfers may take an additional 2–3 days depending on your bank. COD refunds are transferred via NEFT/UPI to your provided bank account.

4. Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled only before dispatch. Once an order has been picked up by our delivery partner, cancellation is not possible.

  • To cancel, WhatsApp your Order ID to +91 9323242591 immediately after placing the order.
  • Prepaid orders cancelled before dispatch will be refunded within 5–7 business days.
  • COD orders cancelled before dispatch — no charges apply.

5. FSSAI Compliance

FSSAI Licensed

BreakFit / R & B Foods operates under FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) licence. Our products comply with FSSAI food safety and labelling standards.

FSSAI guidelines prohibit the return and reuse of opened food products to prevent contamination. Our no-return policy is fully aligned with these regulations.
